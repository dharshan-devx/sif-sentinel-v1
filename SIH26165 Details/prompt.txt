==================================================
EXPECTED SIF / DOMAIN LOGIC
==================================================

The system is NOT supposed to simply determine:

injury happened = SIF
no injury = non-SIF

The concept is SIF POTENTIAL.

A no-injury near miss can still be HIGH SIF potential when:
- there is a credible high-energy/high-consequence hazard
- a critical barrier/control failed or was absent
- exposure was possible
- the event could plausibly produce serious injury/fatality

The intended intelligence structure is:

Activity
   ↓
Hazard
   ↓
Barrier
   ↓
Barrier Failure
   ↓
SIF Potential
   ↓
Life-Saving Rule

The recurring precursor engine should aggregate normalized patterns across reports.

Example:

Maintenance
+
Stored Energy
+
Energy Isolation
+
Isolation Verification Failure

appears repeatedly across reports.

That should be discoverable as a precursor pattern.
